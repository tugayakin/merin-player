Privacy policy

Short version: Merin Player keeps your data on your TV and doesn’t collect anything.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What Merin Player doesn’t do

  • No accounts, no sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with anyone.

What is stored, and where

Everything Merin Player remembers stays in the app’s storage on your TV:

  • The playlists you add: their name and link, or the Xtream Codes server and username. Passwords are stored encrypted with a key that never leaves the TV.
  • Your favourites, the channel and position you were watching, recently used countries, and a copy of your channel list and TV guide so the app starts quickly.

Removing a playlist in Merin Player deletes its data. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Which servers the app connects to

  • Your IPTV provider: the addresses in the playlists you add, to load the channel list, TV guide, movies, series and the video itself.
  • TV guide sources: the guide address included in your playlist, if any, and for playlists without one, public guide files from epgshare01.online for the country you select.
  • Channel logos and posters: loaded from the addresses your provider lists.

These services receive the usual technical information any internet request carries (such as your IP address). They have their own privacy policies; Merin Player has no control over them.

Children

Merin Player doesn’t knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Your TV subscription, on the big screen

Merin Player plays the channels, movies and series from your own IPTV service on your smart TV.

Merin Player is a player only. It doesn’t include or sell any channels, playlists or content, and it isn’t affiliated with any IPTV provider. Use it only with services you are entitled to watch.

Getting started

  1. Open Merin Player and choose Add a playlist.
  2. Pick M3U link and enter the playlist address your provider gave you, or pick Xtream Codes login and enter the server, username and password.
  3. Choose Add playlist. Your channels appear, sorted by country and theme.

You can add several playlists and switch between them from the playlist button at the top.

Using the remote

  • ▲ ▼ move through channels; a preview starts after a moment. OK watches full screen.
  • In full screen, ▲ ▼ or CH + / − change channel and BACK returns to the list.
  • ▶ then OK on a channel, or any colour key, adds it to your favourites.
  • Movies and series: ◀ ▶ skip 10 seconds, OK pauses, ▼ shows the on-screen controls, BACK stops.
  • The Magic Remote pointer and scroll wheel work everywhere.

Questions

My channels don’t play

Merin Player plays what your provider sends. Check that your subscription is active and that the same playlist works elsewhere. Some providers allow only one device at a time.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your TV. See the privacy policy.
